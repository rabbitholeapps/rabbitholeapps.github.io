## Meet Sofia

Sofia is the coach in Referee IQ. She is trained on the flow, nuance, and context of Rugby Union — so you can have a real back-and-forth about even messy situations.

Whether you are a player, coach, fan, or referee, Referee IQ is for:

- **Fast, clear answers** to law questions
- **Seeing the game through a referee’s eyes** so on-field calls make sense
- **“What if” scenarios** when the law book’s black-and-white text is not enough

Unlike a law book, Referee IQ adds the missing layer: **context**. Not just what the law says — why a call was made, or why it was not.

Daily challenges use official wording, not trivia. The point is judgment, not a chatbot that sounds confident.

## Still in the rabbit hole
