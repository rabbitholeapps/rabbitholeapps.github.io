## Know what is next

Match Calendar is a personal schedule and record for sports officials, built with rugby referees in mind. Agenda puts your next assignment first. Switch to Calendar to browse by date and see the matches on a particular day.

Use **Quick Match** to add the essentials. Open the full details when an assignment needs travel plans, contacts, parking notes, or other information you want close at hand.

## Keep the whole trip together

Store flights, lodging, ground travel, and itinerary items alongside your match. Match-day timelines bring those details into order, so you can see the plan from arrival through kickoff.

For a tournament, keep shared travel and event details together with the individual matches. A local assignment can stay as simple as one match.

## Know what you earned

The **Money** view helps you keep track of paid, unpaid, and donated assignments. Record expenses and reimbursements alongside the work they belong to, and use the After Match flow to close out a completed assignment.

**Insights** brings together your events, positions, recorded mileage, income, and expenses. See what you have worked and what you have kept after costs.

## Take it to the field

Open Match Calendar in your browser and install it on your phone for quick access. Your schedule stays available offline after you have opened it once.

Choose **Try demo** on the sign-in screen to explore sample assignments before adding your own. Enter your assignments from the systems you already use, including MatchReadyTX, and keep your personal travel and money records alongside them.
