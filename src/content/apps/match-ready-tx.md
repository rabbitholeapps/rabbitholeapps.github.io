## How it works

Assigners keep working in Google Sheets. MatchReadyTX syncs that schedule into a mobile app for referees, coaching match officials, team admins, and fans.

1. Teams confirm the fixture — date, time, venue.
2. The assigner finalizes the crew.
3. Officials see the appointment. Teams and fans see names only after the Match Official confirms.

If a club does not confirm, they are not guaranteed a referee. Responsibility sits with the teams playing the match.

## For organizations

This started with Texas (Lone Star Group). The “TX” marks where it began, not a lock-in. Another referee organization can use the same foundation.
