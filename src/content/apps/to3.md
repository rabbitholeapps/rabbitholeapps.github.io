## What it does

**Live audio.** Talk to your crew in real time. Clear sound, simple controls. Everyone talks and hears at once. No push-to-talk.

**Events and sessions.** Create an event, add sessions, and see who is hosting or listening. Share a code. People join with one tap.

**Sign in.** Email or Google. Same account on every device.

**Notifications.** Optional alerts when a session you follow goes live.

## Keep it simple

Run everything from the venue. No desktop needed. The microphone is for live rooms. Mute when you are not talking. Black and white, high contrast, readable in bright sun or a dark hall.

**Subscription, not hardware.** We are not selling you a radio. You already have a phone and headphones. Tō3 is subscription-based. Pay for the live voice, not a gear bag.

## Why we built it

Justin and Marquise both come from rugby: playing, coaching, and officiating, from parks games through Major League Rugby and international 7s. The wall was always the same: three officials, trying to coordinate, with no good way to talk.

Broadcast radios cost hundreds of dollars. Shouting and hand signals only go so far. Tō3 is bring-your-own-headphones: tap, connect, go. Latency under 300 milliseconds.

We started with rugby crews. The same need shows up for tour guides, stage crews, coaches on multi-field days, and race officials. Anyone who has to stay in sync while moving.

Spectators can listen in. Public and transparent by default, because accountability is part of the job.
