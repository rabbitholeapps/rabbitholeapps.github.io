# Delete your RefLog data

RefLog is **local-first** and does not require an account. Match data lives on your device, not in the cloud.

## How to delete local data

**Option 1: Delete individual matches**

1. Open the match list in Ref Mode or Coach Mode
2. Swipe left on a match, or long-press to select
3. Tap delete
4. Confirm

**Option 2: Uninstall the app**

Uninstall RefLog on iOS or Android. All local match data is removed.

Once deleted, local data cannot be recovered. Export anything you want to keep first.

## Before you delete

1. Open the match in RefLog
2. Tap export
3. Save the PDF or Excel file
4. Send it to email, Drive, Dropbox, or wherever you keep records
