# Support

Questions or feedback about **Tō3**? Email [rabbitholeapps26@gmail.com](mailto:rabbitholeapps26@gmail.com) with the subject **Tō3 Support**.

We read every message. Typical reply time is 24–48 hours.

Please include:

- Device type (iOS or Android) and OS version
- Tō3 app version (from in-app settings or about screen, if shown)
- A short description of the issue
- Screenshots if they help

## Getting started

1. **Install** Tō3 from Google Play (and from the App Store when the listing is live).
2. **Sign in** with Google or Sign in with Apple.
3. **Create or join an event**, then open a **live room** when you are ready to go on air.

If something fails on first launch, try updating the app and restarting your device, then sign in again.

## Common questions

**What is Tō3 for?**  
Tō3 is for **live, full-duplex voice** around events — crews, officials, hosts, and listeners staying connected in real time.

**Why does the app need the microphone?**  
The microphone is used when you **join or host a live audio session**. You can leave the session or mute when you are not speaking.

**Do I need an account?**  
Yes. Tō3 uses **Firebase Authentication** so your profile and events can sync across devices.

**How do subscriptions work?**  
Some features require a paid plan. Purchases are completed on our website via Stripe — not inside the App Store or Google Play in-app purchase flow. See the [Terms of Use](../terms/) and [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) for billing details.

**How do I delete my account and data?**  
Use **Profile → Delete account** in the app, or follow the steps on [Delete account](../delete-account/).
