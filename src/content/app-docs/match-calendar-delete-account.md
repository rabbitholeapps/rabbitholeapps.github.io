# Delete your Match Calendar data

Match Calendar stores your personal schedule in **Google Cloud Firestore** when you are signed in. You can delete data from the app or by emailing us.

## Option 1: Delete only Match Calendar data (recommended)

This removes your matches, tournaments, and Calendar preferences. Your Google or Apple sign-in and any **MatchReadyTX** society profile stay intact.

1. Sign in at [matchcalendar.web.app](https://matchcalendar.web.app)
2. Open **Profile** from the masthead avatar
3. Under **Your data**, choose **Delete my Match Calendar data**
4. Confirm the action

## Option 2: Delete profile and account

This removes:

- All Match Calendar data
- Your shared `users/{uid}` profile document for this Firebase project
- Your Firebase Authentication account for this project

It does **not** automatically remove MatchReadyTX organization membership or assignment history. Those may still exist until removed in MatchReadyTX.

1. Sign in
2. Open **Profile**
3. Under **Delete account**, choose **Delete my profile & account**
4. Type **DELETE** to confirm

You will be signed out everywhere this Firebase project is used until you sign in again.

## Option 3: Clear local or demo data

If you are not signed in, or you used **Try demo**, data may exist only in your browser.

1. Open **Profile**
2. Choose **Clear all local data**, or clear site data in your browser settings

Local data cannot be recovered once cleared.

## Option 4: Email us

Email [justinxhale@gmail.com](mailto:justinxhale@gmail.com?subject=Match%20Calendar%20delete%20request) from the address tied to your account. Tell us whether you want Calendar-only deletion or full account deletion. We verify the request and respond within 30 days.

## Before you delete

Export or note anything you need for tax, reimbursement, or society records. Deletion is permanent.
