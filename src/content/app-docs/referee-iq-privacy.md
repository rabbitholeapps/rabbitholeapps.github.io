# Privacy Policy for Referee IQ

_Last updated: 24 August 2026_

Referee IQ (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Referee IQ mobile app.

Referee IQ is still in development and is not in public stores yet. This policy describes how the app is built to handle data when you use it.

## 1. Information we collect

When you use Referee IQ, we may collect:

- **Account information:** email address, display name (if you provide one), authentication tokens
- **Device information:** device model, OS version, IP address, app version
- **Usage data:** actions in the app, screens you view, session length and frequency
- **Crash and performance data:** crash logs and diagnostics

## 2. How we collect it

- **Firebase Authentication** (Google Sign-In or email)
- **Cloud Firestore** (account and app data)
- **Google Analytics for Firebase** (usage)

Google may also process information under [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. How we use it

- Authenticate and manage your account
- Provide and improve features
- Diagnose issues and keep the app reliable
- Respond to support requests

We **do not sell or rent** personal information.

## 4. Storage and retention

Information is stored in Google Firebase and Google Cloud. We keep it until you ask us to delete it.

## 5. Your choices and account deletion

Email [rabbitholeapps26@gmail.com](mailto:rabbitholeapps26@gmail.com) to delete your account and associated data.

We verify the request and delete within **30 days**. An in-app delete option is planned.

See [Delete account](../delete-account/) for the full steps.

## 6. Third-party services

- Firebase Authentication
- Cloud Firestore
- Google Analytics for Firebase

## 7. Security

- Encryption in transit (HTTPS)
- Secure authentication
- Storage on trusted cloud infrastructure

No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date.
