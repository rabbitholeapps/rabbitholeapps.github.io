# Support

Questions or feedback? Email [JustinXHale@gmail.com](mailto:JustinXHale@gmail.com) with the subject **RefLog Support**.

We read every message. Typical reply time is 24–48 hours.

Please include:

- Device type (iOS or Android) and OS version
- RefLog version (Settings → About)
- What you were trying to do
- Screenshots if they help

## Getting started

RefLog includes interactive walkthroughs. If you dismissed them, turn tutorials back on in Settings.

## Common questions

**How do I unlock Tech Zone?**  
Tech Zone is a beta for advanced users. Tap the RefLog banner in the app drawer seven times. After that you can toggle it in Settings.

**How do I export a match report?**  
After you log a match in Ref Mode or an observation in Coach Mode, use export to generate a PDF. Save it on the device or share it.

**Does RefLog work offline?**  
Yes. Everything is stored on the device. No internet required.

**How do I back up my data?**  
Data lives on the device. Export matches as PDF or Excel regularly. Cloud sync is planned.

**Can I use RefLog for other sports?**  
It is rugby-specific today. If you want another sport, tell us.
