## Getting started

1. Open [Match Calendar](https://matchcalendar.web.app)
2. Sign in with Google or Apple, or choose **Try demo** to explore sample assignments
3. Use **Quick Match** to add an assignment, or open **Full details** for travel and pay fields
4. Install the app to your home screen from the sign-in or About screen when your browser offers it

## Common questions

**Is Match Calendar the same as MatchReadyTX?**  
No. MatchReadyTX is for referee societies and assigners. Match Calendar is your **personal** schedule and records. You can use the same sign-in because both share a Firebase project.

**Can I use Match Calendar offline?**  
After you have opened your schedule while online, Firestore local cache and the PWA shell help you read and edit offline. Changes sync when you reconnect.

**How do I delete my data?**  
Sign in, open **Profile**, and choose **Delete my Match Calendar data** or **Delete my profile & account**. See [Delete account](../delete-account/) for details.
