# Delete your Tō3 account and data

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Tō3 uses a **cloud account** (Firebase Authentication) and stores **profile and event-related data** in our backend so the service can work across devices. This page explains how to remove that data.

## Option 1: Delete in the app (recommended)

1. Open **Profile** in Tō3.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Read the warning, then confirm by typing **DELETE**.

This permanently removes your Firebase Authentication account and Tō3 profile, cancels active billing, and removes your account identifier from active event, participant, download-ticket, and organization records. It also **forfeits** any remaining paid time, as described in our [Privacy Policy](../privacy/) and [Terms of Use](../terms/). The shared-event and recording exceptions are described below.

## Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in, email [rabbitholeapps26@gmail.com](mailto:rabbitholeapps26@gmail.com?subject=T%C5%8D3%20account%20deletion) with:

- The **email address** tied to your Tō3 login
- Confirmation that you want your **account and associated data** deleted (profile, account identifiers in event records, and similar app data)

We process verified requests as soon as we reasonably can. Some records may be retained where **law or security** requires (for example, short-lived logs).

## What deleting your account affects

Removing your account means you will **lose access** to:

- Your **Tō3 profile** (display name, optional photo, preferences you stored with us)
- **Events and sessions** tied to your account, subject to the shared-event handling and legal exceptions below

If you created a shared event, Tō3 transfers operational ownership to an eligible remaining host when possible. If no eligible successor remains, Tō3 archives the event and anonymizes its creator. Your account identifier is removed from active event and organization records.

If you were the sole host of an event, Tō3 stops any recording still in progress and deletes that event's stored MP3 before completing account deletion. If an event had other hosts, its shared recording may remain available to eligible remaining hosts. We remove your account identifiers from the shared event, but its room-composite audio may still contain your **voice** if you spoke while recording was active.

To request exceptional removal of a shared recording, email [rabbitholeapps26@gmail.com](mailto:rabbitholeapps26@gmail.com?subject=T%C5%8D3%20Recording%20Removal) with enough event information for us to locate it.

**Live sessions** you hosted may also end or become unavailable to other participants once the account is removed, depending on timing and technical constraints.

## Before you delete

- If you need a **record** of something (schedules, contacts), save it outside the app first. We may not be able to recover data after deletion.
- If you only want to **stop using** Tō3, you can **sign out** and uninstall; that does not automatically delete cloud data. Use the steps above if you want the account removed.
- To **cancel a subscription** without deleting your account, use the billing management link in the app or on our website.
