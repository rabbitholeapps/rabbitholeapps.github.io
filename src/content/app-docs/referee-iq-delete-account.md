# Delete your Referee IQ account

To permanently delete your Referee IQ account and associated data, follow the steps below. Once deleted, the account and data **cannot be recovered**.

## How to request deletion

Email us from the address linked to your Referee IQ account:

- **To:** [rabbitholeapps26@gmail.com](mailto:rabbitholeapps26@gmail.com?subject=Referee%20IQ%20Account%20Deletion%20Request&body=Please%20delete%20my%20Referee%20IQ%20account.%0A%0ARegistered%20email%3A%20%3Cyour%20email%3E%0A%0AI%20understand%20this%20action%20is%20permanent.%0A)
- **Subject:** `Referee IQ Account Deletion Request`
- **Include:** the **registered email** for the account, and a statement that you want it deleted

## What we delete

- Account profile (email and display name)
- Authentication data
- App usage data tied to the account
- Other personal data stored in **Cloud Firestore**

We keep little data and **do not sell** personal information.

## Timeline

We verify the request and complete deletion **within 30 days**. You will get a confirmation email when it is done.

## In-app deletion

An in-app delete option is planned. Until then, email deletion is fully supported.

## Need help?

Reply to your deletion email, or write [rabbitholeapps26@gmail.com](mailto:rabbitholeapps26@gmail.com).
